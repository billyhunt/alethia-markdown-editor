Alethia

Paper ground, hard black rules, one hot orange. Nothing is softened, nothing is hidden: emphasis is a fill, structure is a rule, and the chrome speaks in small mono capitals.

This page shows the system live, drawn by alethia.css. Switch the theme in the bar above. The rules and the reasons behind them are in the README; the values, for tools, are in tokens.json. The app's own stylesheets are the source of truth for all of it.

Contrast

Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2 ratios, computed here in the browser from the values in alethia.css, for both themes. Text needs 4.5:1 (AA) or 7:1 (AAA). Anything under 4.5 is marked as a fail: all chrome type is small, so the 3:1 allowance for large text never applies here. The last two rows fail on purpose: they are the pairs the token split exists to prevent.

Rules & shape

Structure is drawn, not shaded. Four line weights, square corners everywhere, no shadows. Depth comes from inversion: a band of ink on paper, or paper on ink.
